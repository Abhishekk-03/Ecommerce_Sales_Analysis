# Ecommerce-sale-Dashboard

**📌 Business Objective**
- A Dynamic Power Bi dashboard designed to analyze and visualize Year-to-Date (YTD) Performance metrics for an ecommerce business across key dimensions.

**📊 KPI (Key Performance Indicator) Requirements**

- TOTAL YTD - The total revenue or sales accumulated from the beginning of the year to date.
- YTD Profit - The net profit earned from the start of the year up to the current date
- Profit Margin - The percentage of revenue that remains as profit
- YTD Quantidy - The total number of units sold or produced year-to-date.

**📈 Key Insight**

- 
- YTD Profit
- Profit Margin
- YTD Quantidy
